## NAVADA Business Model: FAQ


### FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS


### Common questions from CEOs and executives about AI adoption


### STRATEGY & BUSINESS QUESTIONS


### Q: Do we really need AI, or is it just hype?

A: AI is not hype—it's a fundamental shift in how work gets done. Companies not adopting AI will face: 3-5x productivity gaps vs competitors, difficulty attracting talent (who expect AI tools), and potential obsolescence in 3-5 years. The question isn't IF but HOW FAST.


### Q: What's the minimum viable AI investment?

A: For most SMEs, £10-20K gets you started: £5K for consulting/strategy, £5K for initial training, £5-10K for first pilot. This delivers proof of concept and ROI data for larger investment.


### Q: Should we build AI capabilities in-house or outsource?

A: For 90% of companies: USE CLOUD APIs (don't build). Build only if: AI is core to your product, you have unique data advantages, or regulatory requirements demand it. Focus on USING AI well, not building it.


### Q: How do I convince my board to invest in AI?

A: Use this document's financial models. Show: NPV/IRR analysis, competitor benchmarking, risk of inaction (market share loss), and start with a low-risk pilot with measurable ROI.


### Q: What if AI doesn't work for our industry?

A: AI applies to every industry. Even 'traditional' sectors see massive gains: Construction (+25% efficiency), Agriculture (+30% yield optimisation), Manufacturing (+35% quality). Find your use case—it exists.


### COST & FINANCIAL QUESTIONS


### Q: How much does AI actually cost?

A: Cloud AI costs £0.01-0.50 per interaction depending on complexity. A typical employee's AI usage costs £30-100/month. Compare to their salary: AI augmentation costs <1% of payroll for 2-3x productivity gains.


### Q: What's a realistic ROI timeframe?

A: Most AI projects achieve payback in 3-6 months. Full ROI (10x+) typically within 18 months. This is faster than almost any other technology investment.


### Q: Why do some companies overspend on AI?

A: Common mistakes: Using expensive models (Opus) for simple tasks, not implementing caching, keeping full conversation history, no model routing. Following this guide prevents 80% of overspend.


### Q: What hidden costs should I budget for?

A: Human orchestration (£15-20K/year per heavy user), training (£5-10K per cohort), integration (£10-50K depending on systems), and ongoing optimisation (5-10% of AI spend).


### PEOPLE & WORKFORCE QUESTIONS


### Q: Will AI replace my employees?

A: AI augments more than replaces. Expect: 15-30% role elimination (mostly admin/data entry), 60% role transformation (same people, different tasks), 10-25% new roles created. Net impact: slight reduction but higher productivity.


### Q: How do I handle employee anxiety about AI?

A: Transparency is key. Announce reskilling programmes BEFORE AI rollout. Involve employees in pilot selection. Celebrate AI-augmented successes. Those who embrace AI should see career growth, not threat.


### Q: What skills do my people need?

A: Priority skills: Prompt engineering (2-4 weeks to learn), AI output review/editing, Process redesign thinking, Data literacy. These are teachable to any motivated employee.


### Q: Should I hire AI specialists?

A: For most companies: No, not initially. Start with consultants, train existing staff, use managed services. Hire specialists only when AI is core to operations (usually 12-18 months in).


### TECHNICAL QUESTIONS


### Q: Which AI model should we use?

A: For 70% of tasks: Claude Haiku or GPT-4o-mini (cheap, fast, good). For complex analysis: Claude Sonnet (best balance). For critical decisions only: Claude Opus (expensive but exceptional). Use model routing for optimal cost.


### Q: Is our data safe with AI providers?

A: Enterprise AI providers (Anthropic, OpenAI, Azure) do NOT train on your data by default. Use API access (not consumer apps) for business data. Review data processing agreements. Consider EU hosting for GDPR.


### Q: How do we integrate AI with existing systems?

A: Most modern software has AI APIs or plugins. Common patterns: API integration (custom), Zapier/Make (no-code), Microsoft Copilot (Office), Salesforce Einstein (CRM). Start with one system, expand.


### Q: What about AI hallucinations and errors?

A: AI can be wrong. Mitigate with: Human review for critical outputs, fact-checking prompts, RAG (retrieval-augmented generation) for factual accuracy, clear user understanding that AI assists, not replaces judgment.


### UK-SPECIFIC QUESTIONS


### Q: What about UK AI regulations?

A: UK is currently 'pro-innovation' with lighter regulation than EU. Key considerations: GDPR still applies, ICO guidance on AI, sector-specific rules (FCA for finance, etc.). Prepare for future regulation by documenting AI decisions now.


### Q: Can we build our own data centre for AI?

A: Generally not advisable for UK companies. Energy costs 3x US, CO2 regulations restrict new builds, grid capacity limited. Use cloud APIs—it's more cost-effective and compliant.


### Q: How does Brexit affect AI strategy?

A: Key impacts: Different data rules than EU (adequacy decision helps), potential for UK-specific AI regulation, talent pool considerations (visa rules). Strategy: Stay flexible, monitor regulatory changes.
